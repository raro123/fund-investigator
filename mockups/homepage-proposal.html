The Sales Trap

Funds are marketed on their best returns.
Your SIP statement tells a different story.

Returns are marketed. Risk is footnoted.

Past returns are everywhere. Rolling returns and risk-adjusted metrics are hard to find — and that's exactly what you need to decide.

Today's winner is tomorrow's trap.

Rankings reward recent performance. Last year's chart-topper may have simply caught a rising market. Returns without context are a trap disguised as a signal.

So we check the full picture.
