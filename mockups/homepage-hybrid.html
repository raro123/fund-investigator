The Sales Trap

You were shown the best returns.
Nobody showed you the risk you took to get them.

Returns are marketed. Risk is footnoted.

The headline number is everywhere. The drawdowns and risk-adjusted figures — the part that tells you if it's a good fund — are buried.

Last year's winner felt like a safe pick.

It probably just caught a rising market. Without context, a top ranking is a trap disguised as a signal — and you can't tell from the app.
