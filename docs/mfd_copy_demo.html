How it works

Five steps. You approve every investment.

  1. Message us

    Send a WhatsApp message. We reply within 2 working days.

  2. First conversation

    We discuss your goals, your time frame, your existing funds and your risk profile.

  3. Fund shortlisting and selection help

    We shortlist suitable funds and explain why each fits, with its costs and our commission.

  4. We set it up, you approve

    We help from KYC to investment. You approve each one by OTP and pay the fund house directly.

  5. Reviews and service

    We review your funds at regular intervals. We help with SIP changes, switches and redemptions.

Not the right fit? If you prefer to choose funds yourself, use Deepdive and direct plans. If you need a full financial plan, tax or insurance advice, speak to a SEBI-registered investment adviser.
