Most mutual fund claims begin with a return number. That number is useful, but it leaves several questions unanswered.

Did the fund beat a benchmark that reflects what it was allowed to own? Was the result repeated across different market conditions, or concentrated in one year? How much risk did the fund take, how far did it fall, and how long did recovery take?

Fund Investigator examines those questions using a repeatable set of checks.

## What we investigate

Each investigation starts with a named fund, plan, benchmark, analysis period and data date. We then examine five areas:

1. whether the fund beat a fair benchmark;
2. whether the result was consistent or driven by one period;
3. whether the additional return justified the additional risk;
4. how far the fund fell when the market turned; and
5. how long recovery took.

The objective is not to turn those checks into a rating. It is to show what the historical evidence supports, what it contradicts, and what it cannot establish.

Fund Investigator does not sell mutual funds or earn commissions from fund houses. The work is informational and analytical; it is not a recommendation to buy, sell or continue holding a fund.

## Why the complete investigations remain on FundInvestigator.com

Substack is the distribution layer. [FundInvestigator.com](https://fundinvestigator.com/reports/) remains the canonical home of the research.

The full investigation includes the complete narrative, charts, assumptions, source notes and limitations in one place. Keeping that evidence on the site also means a published report has one stable address when it is cited, corrected or updated.

This publication will not reproduce those reports in full.

## What an Investigation Brief contains

An Investigation Brief is a shorter reading of a new investigation or material update. It will usually include:

- the fund, benchmark and analysis period;
- a concise result for all five checks;
- the two or three findings that define the historical record;
- one chart when it materially clarifies the evidence;
- the limits of what the analysis can establish; and
- a link to the complete investigation.

The brief should be useful on its own. The complete report remains available for anyone who wants to inspect the evidence and methodology.

## What subscribers will receive

Subscribers will receive a concise email when a new fund investigation or material update is ready. Methodology notes and material Deepdive updates may also be included when they change how the evidence can be examined.

There will be no daily market commentary, product pitches, personalised suitability conclusions or manufactured publishing schedule. The publication is free, and every email includes an unsubscribe link.

## Where Deepdive fits

[Deepdive](https://deepdive.fundinvestigator.com/) is the interactive analysis tool behind the broader project. It lets an investor select a fund and inspect return, consistency, risk and drawdown evidence using explicit analysis settings.

Deepdive provides the measurements. Fund Investigator publications explain what those measurements show, where they disagree, and which questions remain open. Neither replaces an assessment of an investor's objectives, time horizon or capacity for loss.
