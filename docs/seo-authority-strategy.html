A cadence you can sustain.

One flagship packet every two weeks is enough to build a recognisable body of work without diluting the research.

Days 01–14 / foundation

Make the system legible.

Align the profiles, the promise, the links and the measurement before publishing at volume.

  • Unify profile bios and visual identity
  • Set UTM links and conversion events
  • Define the five recurring series
  • Pin the best investigation on X
Days 15–60 / publish

Prove the point of view.

Ship three research packets. Let the audience see the same method applied across different funds.

  • 2 investigations per month
  • 1 Substack essay per packet
  • 1 X thread plus 3–5 posts weekly
  • 2 YouTube films plus Shorts monthly
Days 61–90 / compound

Turn attention into proof.

Use the first signals to choose the next questions and create external reasons to cite the work.

  • Publish a fund comparison
  • Invite one independent guest
  • Release an original data asset
  • Review Search, email and video metrics
